1. What this tool is

This tool helps you obtain a copy of your own medical record from your health system and save it to your own device. It is operated by JT Perry (jt.perry@gmail.com) as an independent individual.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Epic Systems Corporation, athenahealth, or any health system. References to those organizations are descriptive only.

2. How your data flows

This is a browser-only application. When you connect:

  • You authenticate directly with your health system, on their own site. Your username and password are never seen by this site.
  • Your browser exchanges the authorization code and requests your record directly from your health system's servers.
  • Your record is assembled in your browser and written to your device.

Your health information is never transmitted to, received by, or stored on any server operated by us. This is a property of how the software is built, not merely a policy commitment. It also means we cannot retrieve, correct, or delete your record — we never hold a copy.

“do not impose any restrictions on how an individual or the individual’s designee, such as an app, may use the health information that has been disclosed pursuant to the individual’s right of access.”

You accept sole and complete responsibility for the confidentiality, integrity, security, storage, use, and disclosure of the copy you create, and for any consequence of it being exposed — including through a lost or unencrypted device, a cloud-sync folder, malware, another person with access to your computer, or your own sharing of the file with any person or service.

Health information cannot be un-disclosed. If you are not prepared to secure this file yourself, do not use this tool.
